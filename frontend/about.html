The PawHaven Journey

Born From a Love for Bangladesh's Animals

Too often, pet owners in Bangladesh struggle with expired food, counterfeit brands, and delayed deliveries. We launched PawHaven BD to provide a dependable, transparent platform where pet well-being always comes before profit.

Today, we serve thousands of households across Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet, and beyond—helping pets live vibrant, playful, and healthier lives.
