Yes, 100%. PawHaven BD strictly rejects loose or repacked foods. All bags arrive in original factory-sealed bags with complete barcode verification and authorized importer seals.

Yes! Cash on Delivery is available across all 64 districts in Bangladesh. You may inspect the outer package before paying the delivery agent.

We provide a 7-day hassle-free exchange or return for any sealed, unopened items or goods damaged during transport. Contact our hotline immediately upon receipt.
